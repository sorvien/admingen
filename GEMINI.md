# Project Rules & Assistant Instructions

## Social Media & Content Management Protocol

Whenever the user asks for social media content, post ideas, or marketing copy:

1. **Check Previous History First**:
   - Before drafting any post, ALWAYS inspect [`social_media_history.md`](./social_media_history.md) and [`content_calendar.md`](./content_calendar.md).
   - Verify previously used hooks, topics, and published dates.
   - Do not repeat recently used angles, hooks, or topics unless deliberately doing a follow-up part 2/recap.

2. **Drafting Guidelines**:
   - Tailor copy specifically for developer audiences (TypeScript, Bun, ElysiaJS, Drizzle ORM).
   - Keep copy high-signal, punchy, and free of generic AI buzzwords ("In today's fast-paced world...", "Revolutionize your workflow...", etc.).
   - Include specific platform-tailored formats (e.g. short punchy hooks for X/Twitter, structured breakdowns for LinkedIn).

3. **Post Logging & Maintenance**:
   - When the user confirms they have posted, scheduled, or approved content, immediately log the entry into [`social_media_history.md`](./social_media_history.md) under the table and archive section.
   - Move or mark any corresponding item in [`content_calendar.md`](./content_calendar.md) as scheduled/published.
